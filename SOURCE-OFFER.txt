Written Offer for Corresponding Source (GNU GPL v3)
===================================================

shairkindle bundles ONE GPLv3-or-later binary:

  * extension/bin/fbink — NiLuJe's FBInk CLI (https://github.com/NiLuJe/FBInk).

FBInk is distributed and executed as a SEPARATE program; shairkindle does not link
against libfbink. shairkindle's own original code is licensed under MIT (see LICENSE),
while this separately distributed FBInk executable remains covered by GPLv3-or-later.
Other third-party components carry their own licenses -- see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md.

Where the corresponding source is
---------------------------------
The complete Corresponding Source for extension/bin/fbink -- including any build/install
scripts needed to build and run it -- is FBInk upstream at commit
83110d3d278cf9cd44cc1d16237e284a89f72633 (FBInk v1.25.0-535-g83110d3d), published at
https://github.com/NiLuJe/FBInk (check out that commit), and also mirrored in
best-effort-labs/kinduino at fbinkd/third_party/FBInk (a git submodule pinned to that
exact commit; kinduino v0.5.2-30-g091891f, from whose fbinkd/fbink.armv6 this binary
was taken). The bundled binary is that build artifact with debug/symbol sections
removed (`arm-none-eabi-strip -s`); the Corresponding Source and the kinduino fbinkd
build recipe (Makefile + toolchain config) are unchanged by that step.

The offer
---------
To anyone who possesses this fbink binary, we offer to provide the complete
machine-readable Corresponding Source under GPLv3, either by free download from a network
server or, on request, on a durable physical medium for no more than our reasonable cost
of physically providing it. This offer is valid for at least three years from our last
distribution of this binary. The source is already available online at no cost (above);
this written offer satisfies GPLv3 §6 for anyone who received the binary without it.
Request via besteffortlabs@heliodox.com.
